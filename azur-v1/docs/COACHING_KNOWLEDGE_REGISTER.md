# AZUR Coaching Knowledge Register — V0.1

Status: research-backed design input; **not** an automated prescription policy. This document is a curated synthesis, not a reproduction of any book or training plan.

## Evidence and decision principles

| ID | Coaching principle | Evidence/source | Proposed AZUR use | Safeguard |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FRI-01 | Each key workout should have a clear purpose connected to the athlete's goals and season. | Joe Friel, *Preparing for the 2010 Season, Part 1*, https://www.trainingbible.com/joesblog/2009/10/preparing-for-2010-season-part-1.html | Explain the intended session purpose in the Coaching tab. | Do not claim a physiological adaptation was achieved merely because a workout was scheduled or completed. |
| FRI-02 | Training generally becomes more race-specific as the priority race approaches. | Joe Friel, *K.I.S.S. Periodization*, https://joefrieltraining.com/kiss-periodization/ | Explain why session content differs between base, build, taper and race-specific periods, when the phase is known. | Do not infer phase from an individual workout; require a recorded phase and race goal. |
| FRI-03 | Weekly training design depends on race goals, current phase and key abilities. | Joe Friel, *Training Week Design*, https://joefrieltraining.com/training-week-design/ | Assess whether a session supports an identified athlete limiter and the week's goals. | Do not label a limiter without validated assessment/history. |
| FRI-04 | Suitable training depends on experience, available hours, phase and measurement equipment. | Joe Friel, *Training Plans*, https://joefrieltraining.com/training-plans/ | Explain prescriptions relative to the athlete's available time and confirmed benchmarks. | Missing benchmarks are not estimated from unrelated metrics. |
| FRI-05 | Recovery and training flexibility matter; stress is not endlessly increased. | Joe Friel, *Preparing for the 2010 Season, Part 1*, https://www.trainingbible.com/joesblog/2009/10/preparing-for-2010-season-part-1.html | Coach-review prompts when athlete-reported difficulty and recent training patterns warrant investigation. | Duration changes and isolated RPE are not diagnoses of fatigue, injury or readiness. |

## Acceptance criteria for a future coaching rule

1. Explicit source and principle, with review date and evidence limitations.
2. Required athlete inputs identified (sport, goal, phase, benchmark, training history, subjective feedback).
3. Unknown/missing inputs produce cautious guidance, not fabricated values.
4. Device-derived and athlete-reported observations remain distinct.
5. Coach approval for significant volume/intensity changes.
6. Tests for beginner, experienced, missing data, inconsistent data and cross-sport cases.
7. A short, useful athlete-facing explanation rather than a lengthy generic paragraph.

## Implementation status

The workout Coaching tab currently interprets explicit interval targets and reported RPE. The source principles above are **not yet wired into a decision engine**. Future changes must be separately implemented, tested and verified on staging.
